Positioning

Senior Growth Manager at Broom, where I lead a twelve-person cross-functional team across two financing products in Indonesia’s used-car market. Six years scaling acquisition, activation and conversion in high-consideration journeys — the kind where the decision takes weeks, the funnel crosses four organisations, and the revenue lands at the very end.

The through-line is that I build what I measure. A leasing-channeling operating system that carried Rp975 billion.
